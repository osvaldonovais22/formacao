# Traction ranking: AI secretaries for doctors in Brazil (state as of Nov 2026)

Scope note: about 15 searches and fetches. Almost all material that names products is vendor-authored. Independent signals (press articles, funding announcements, Reclame Aqui) are thin and spotty. No independent "best AI secretary for doctors" ranking was found.

## Which products appear in independent comparisons, analyst reports and medical-community discussions?

### Takeaway
No independent, analyst-grade ranking of AI secretaries for doctors exists in Brazil as of Nov 2026. The "melhores" lists are written by vendors or affiliates. Independent comparisons cover generic WhatsApp chatbot tools (ChatGuru, Take Blip, BotConversa, etc.), not medical AI secretaries. The names that recur across vendor pages, press and search results are Cloudia, Secretária IA (usesecretariaia.com), Clinia, Healz "Carol", Syntia, FF.AI, Axônio, NexusMed and GeraCare. Incumbents (Doctoralia/Noa, Afya iClinic, Feegow) appear mainly with clinical-note AI, not a patient-facing WhatsApp agent.

### Cited Findings
- The top search results for "secretária virtual IA para médicos" are all vendor pages: FF.AI, Secretária IA (usesecretariaia), Agência do Médico, Cloudia, Healz (Carol), Secretária Inteligente (secretariamedica.com.br) and Syntia — [FF.AI](https://ffai.ia.br/ia-para-whatsapp-de-clinica); [Secretária IA](https://usesecretariaia.com/secretaria-virtual-para-consultorio-medico/); [Agência do Médico](https://agenciadomedico.com.br/secretaria-com-ia-para-medicos/); [Cloudia](https://cloudia.com.br/); [Healz Carol](https://healz.com.br/produtos/secretaria-virtual-ia-para-medicos/); [Secretária Inteligente](https://secretariamedica.com.br/ia/); [Syntia](https://syntiamed.com/)
- Other vendors that surface: Axônio ("Secretária com IA para médicos"), NexusMed, GeraCare (CRM with AI), Verboo — [Axônio](https://axoniomed.com.br/); [NexusMed](https://nexusmed.tech/); [GeraCare](https://geracare.com.br/secretaria-virtual-com-ia); [Verboo](https://verboo.ai/pt/blog/ia-whatsapp-clinicas-faltas-agendamentos)
- The closest thing to an independent comparison is zapfunil, which calls itself an "independent portal" but earns affiliate commissions from some of the products. Its "Melhores chatbots para clínicas no WhatsApp (2026)" (updated Sep 2026) ranks generic chatbot platforms, not medical AI secretaries: ChatGuru, Take Blip, SendPulse, BotConversa, Kommo, Wati and ManyChat. Octadesk, Zenvia, RD Station Conversas, 360dialog and Positus are excluded — [zapfunil](https://zapfunil.com/comparativos/melhores-chatbots-para-clinicas-no-whatsapp)
- zapfunil (Sep 2026) also reports that none of the 7 platforms it reviewed documents a connector to iClinic, Doctoralia, TuoTempo, Clinicorp, Amplimed or Ninsaúde. Vendors such as Axônio, FF.AI, NexusMed and Clinia claim API integrations with Feegow, iClinic and Doctoralia — [zapfunil](https://zapfunil.com/comparativos/melhores-chatbots-para-clinicas-no-whatsapp); [Clinia Feegow integration page](https://clinia.io/integracoes/feegow); [Secretária IA + Feegow](https://usesecretariaia.com/integracao-feeegow/)
- An EUVENDO Saúde blog on "Alternativas à secretária virtual para médicos" (04/08/2026) names categories only, no products (generative AI voice/WhatsApp agents, hybrid SaaS, management software with automation, human-assisted service). The site appears to have a commercial interest — [EUVENDO Saúde](https://euvendosaude.com/blog/alternativas-a-secretaria-virtual-para-medicos/)
- Startups to Watch lists Cloudia as an "AI virtual secretary for clinics" (catalogue listing, not a ranking) — [startupstowatch](https://startupstowatch.com.br/produto/cloudia)
- Analyst reports, Liga Ventures: no 2026 report specifically on AI scheduling or AI secretaries. The Liga/Unimed FESP study found "buscadores e agendamentos" was 6–8% of healthtechs founded 2020–2025. A Liga landscape counted 130 Brazilian healthtechs using AI (chatbots, triage, engagement). Liga counted 43 healthtech deals in 2025 (41 in 2024), with total value down 28% to R$ 1.214 bi — [Medicina S/A](https://medicinasa.com.br/healthtechs-medsa26/); [Futuro da Saúde](https://futurodasaude.com.br/investimentos-healthtechs-2026/); [Liga Ventures](https://liga.ventures/insights/artigos/como-a-ia-esta-transformando-a-experiencia-do-paciente/); [Saúde Business](https://www.saudebusiness.com/artigos/estudo-analisa-desenvolvimento-de-healthtechs-no-brasil/)
- Medical-community discussion: no Reddit or forum threads naming Cloudia, Secretária IA or Clinia were found. Trade press covers caution: doctors' resistance to AI, and the TIC Saúde 2024 finding that 18% of establishments use AI, mostly for admin work. It also covers the privacy risk of non-homologated WhatsApp confirmation — [Medicina S/A opinion](https://medicinasa.com.br/?p=173629); [Medicina S/A WhatsApp privacy](https://medicinasa.com.br/consulta-whatsapp)
- Afya's educational content for private-practice doctors ("Sua secretária agenda ou apenas responde WhatsApp?") shows that the incumbent frames this as a pain point — [Afya Educação Médica](https://educacaomedica.afya.com.br/meu-consultorio-particular/raio-x-secretaria)

### Inferences
- The niche is fragmented and early. There is no category leader with independent validation, and visibility is driven by SEO and vendor content marketing.
- By how often they recur across independent and quasi-independent signals (press, funding, Reclame Aqui volume, catalogues), the most "established" dedicated players are Cloudia, Clinia and Secretária IA.
- Generic WhatsApp chatbot platforms (ChatGuru, Take Blip, BotConversa) are what independent reviewers actually compare. They are indirect competitors for the solo doctor's budget.

### Gaps
- No Distrito report on this niche was found. The Liga Ventures Health Techs mapping (263 startups, 18 categories) is gated behind a form.
- No independent review sites (Capterra/G2-type) with review volumes for these products were found.
- No physician-forum sentiment data was found.

## What traction evidence exists for each (funding, customers, revenue, press, reviews)?

### Takeaway
Hard traction data exists for only a few companies. Clinia (R$ 2M pre-seed, 300+ clinics, Nov 2024) and Doutor-AI (US$ 1M pre-seed, enterprise focus) have disclosed funding. Cloudia claims 1,000+ clinics but has a growing Reclame Aqui complaint trail. Secretária IA has press but no disclosed numbers. Doctoralia's Noa Notes has the largest disclosed doctor base (9k+), but it is a clinical scribe, not a patient-facing secretary.

### Cited Findings

**Cloudia (dedicated; clinics and consultórios, multi-channel)**
- Claims to serve 1,000+ clinics in Brazil and Portugal. This is a self-reported marketing figure — [Cloudia](https://cloudia.com.br/ia-para-atendimento-ao-paciente/); [Cloudia](https://cloudia.com.br/)
- Channels: WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and site, 24h. Plans are Pro (with AI) and Elite (adds integration with management systems such as Google Agenda, Clinicorp and Dental Office). Claims 40+ management-system integrations — [Cloudia planos](https://cloudia.com.br/planos/)
- Reclame Aqui: no reputation rating yet (fewer than 10 evaluated complaints). Average response time is 4 days 4 hours (Jan–Jun 2026). The compare page shows 14 complaints and a 5-day average response time in the last 6 months. Complaint themes are slow onboarding (one says more than 2 months), a service not delivered after a month, refund refusals, and recurring robot failures (Aug 2025) — [RA profile](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/empresa/cloudia/lista-reclamacoes/); [RA compare](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/compare/cloudia/); [RA complaint](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/cloudia/servico-de-secretaria-por-ia-nao-entregue-apos-um-mes-e-suporte-demorado-no-whatsapp_st_d2mZjR_3GbO1H/); [RA complaint](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/cloudia/empresa-de-ia-para-consultorios-nao-entrega-o-servico-nao-da-suporte-e-se-recusa-a-devolver-o-dinheiro_u2gZwd5NzZL_0x-C/); [RA robot issues](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/cloudia/problemas-recorrentes-no-funcionamento-do-robo-da-cloudia_g6-H9TlpQxSmSKF0/)
- No funding round found.

**Clinia (dedicated; Porto Alegre, founded 2021)**
- R$ 2M pre-seed led by ABSeed Ventures (first investment of its Seed 3 fund). As of Nov 22, 2024: about 150 brands and 300+ clinics. Named clients include Unimed, Clínica da Cidade, Orthodontic, Instituto Oncológico DeVita and Radi-imagem. AI agent on WhatsApp and Instagram, integrated with management systems — [IT Forum, 22/11/2024](https://itforum.com.br/noticias/clinia-startup-ia-saude-aporte-2-milhoes/)
- Says it books directly into the Feegow agenda from the conversation — [Clinia](https://clinia.io/integracoes/feegow)
- Do not confuse it with Clinia (Montreal, Canada), which raised a US$ 10M Series A from TELUS Ventures — [startupintros](https://startupintros.com/orgs/clinia)

**Secretária IA (usesecretariaia.com; dedicated; doctors and dentists, WhatsApp)**
- Healthtech led by CEO Tarek Ialmam Silva and CTO Caio Emiliano Rodrigues. Selected among the Top 1,000 of Prêmio Sebrae Startups and exhibited at Startup Summit 2026 (Florianópolis). Claims "anti-hallucination" technology and long-term memory. No client, revenue or funding figures were disclosed — [Economia SC, 30/06/2026](https://economiasc.com/2026/06/30/healthtech-secretaria-ia-leva-ao-startup-summit-ia-que-aumenta-conversao-de-clinicas-medicas/)
- Integrates with Google Agenda and Feegow (vendor claim). Some features were marked "em breve (Q1 2026)" — [Secretária IA](https://usesecretariaia.com/home-page/); [Secretária IA Feegow](https://usesecretariaia.com/integracao-feeegow/)

**Doutor-AI (enterprise-focused AI agents; not solo-doctor)**
- US$ 1M pre-seed led by Airborne Ventures. Founder Maurício Honorato. About 10 clients including Conexa, Leve Saúde and Rede D'Or. 700+ trained agents. Focused on health plans, hospitals and large clinic or lab networks, with agents plugged into WhatsApp. Sources disagree on the founding date (2024 vs "February") — [Baguete](https://www.baguete.com.br/noticias/doutor-ai-capta-us-1-mi-com-airborne-ventures); [Mobile Time, 17/04/2025](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/17/04/2025/doutor-ai/); [Startups.com.br](https://startups.com.br/negocios/healthtechs/doutor-ai-aposta-em-ia-para-transformar-a-jornada-do-paciente/)

**Tolki (WhatsApp AI agents for patient reactivation)**
- Press release-style article (Oct 2026): 52k patients "captured" (reactivated to booking) and R$ 8M saved for clinics. Self-reported — [Estado de Minas, Oct 2026](https://www.em.com.br/mundo-corporativo/2026/10/7528143-tolki-capta-52-mil-pacientes-e-poupa-r-8-milhoes-a-clinicas.html)

**Healz "Carol" (AI plus human secretary hybrid)**
- Vendor says it was developed at InovaHC (Hospital das Clínicas SP) and designed around CFM Resolution 2.454/2026. The current version serves a single professional, a direct solo-doctor fit. There is a human secretary backstop for sensitive cases. No independent confirmation of the InovaHC link was found — [Healz](https://healz.com.br/produtos/secretaria-virtual-ia-para-medicos/)

**Syntia, FF.AI, Axônio, NexusMed, GeraCare, Agência do Médico, Secretária Inteligente**
- Vendor pages only. No funding, customer counts or press found — see URLs above.
- Reclame Aqui has a complaint about "cobrança indevida de serviço de secretária por IA após cancelamento" filed under "DR IA I Tecnologias Digitais Ltda" (product brand unclear) — [Reclame Aqui](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/dr-ia-i-tecnologias-digitais-ltda/cobranca-indevida-de-servico-de-secretaria-por-ia-apos-solicitacao-de-cancelamento_CwZFAIM8R41xPnp-/)

**Incumbent platforms**
- Doctoralia (DocPlanner): its AI line in Brazil launched with Noa Notes (Nov 2024), an ambient clinical-note scribe, not a WhatsApp secretary. Doctoralia reports 9k+ doctors and 335k+ consultations in Brazil (an earlier release said 7k doctors and 200k consultations) — [Saúde Digital News, 29/11/2024](https://saudedigitalnews.com.br/29/11/2024/doctoralia-lanca-assistente-ia-para-transformar-a-rotina-dos-medicos/); [Doctoralia press](https://press.doctoralia.com.br/435369-ia-humaniza-consultas-medicas-e-alivia-rotina-de-profissionais-de-saude); [Medicina S/A](https://medicinasa.com.br/noa-notes-doctoralia/)

- Afya iClinic: AI is "Afya iClinic Assist" (transcription and history summary). WhatsApp confirmation is a standard Afya-number reminder, not marketed as AI — [conclinica](https://conclinica.com.br/melhores-sistemas-para-clinicas/); [doctte](https://www.doctte.com/blog/7-melhores-alternativas-ao-iclinic-e-feegow-em-2026-precos)

**Adjacent AI-for-doctor funding (not secretaries, but competing for the same doctor wallet)**
- Telepatia AI (clinical copilot and scribe): US$ 9M seed (Oct 2025) and a US$ 33M Series A led by a16z (Jun 2026), US$ 42M total. Target is 20k Brazilian doctors by the end of 2026 — [Saúde Digital News](https://saudedigitalnews.com.br/10/10/2025/startup-telepatia-de-ia-para-medicos-recebe-us-9-milhoes-para-expandir-ao-brasil/); [HiHub](https://hihub.news/2026/06/18/telepatia-ai-capta-us-33-milhoes-em-serie-a-liderada-pela-andreessen-horowitz-a16z/)

- dr.consulta (clinic network, in-house AI agents): over R$ 1.2M/month in revenue attributed to AI agents and about 75% opex savings (self-reported to Exame) — [Exame](https://exame.com/inteligencia-artificial/exclusivo-dr-consulta-gera-r-12-milhao-por-mes-com-agentes-de-inteligencia-artificial/)

### Inferences
- Ranking by independent traction signals (funding, third-party press, complaint volume as a proxy for customer base, catalogue presence):
  1. **Cloudia**: largest claimed base (1,000+ clinics), most Reclame Aqui volume (implies real paying customers), long-running brand (an old site exists). Its focus is clinics, not solo doctors, and the onboarding and support complaints are an opening.
  2. **Clinia**: only dedicated player with disclosed VC funding plus a named client base (300+ clinics, 2024). Its focus is multi-clinic brands, less solo.
  3. **Secretária IA (usesecretariaia)**: most explicitly aimed at doctors and dentists on WhatsApp, with visible 2026 press and SEO presence, but no hard numbers.
  4. **Healz Carol**: closest product fit to a solo doctor (single-professional, CFM 2.454/2026 positioning, human backstop), but no traction evidence.
  - Incumbent threat: **Doctoralia/Feegow (DocPlanner)** owns the solo doctor's agenda (9k+ Noa users) and could ship a patient-facing agent. Today its AI is a scribe.
- Suggested top-3 competitors for Maestro: Cloudia, Secretária IA and Healz Carol (solo-doctor fit). Doctoralia is the incumbent to watch. Clinia is the alternative to Healz if a funding-backed comparator is preferred.

### Gaps
- No revenue, ARR or growth data for any dedicated player.
- Customer counts are self-reported. Clinia's figure is two years old.
- Social following, Google review counts and app-store data were not collected (out of tool budget).
- Reclame Aqui profiles for Secretária IA, Clinia, Healz and Syntia were not checked.

## Are there notable 2025-2026 entrants or acquisitions in Brazil?

### Takeaway
2025-2026 brought a wave of small, mostly unfunded entrants positioned on CFM Resolution 2.454/2026 and WhatsApp. No acquisition of an AI-secretary startup was found.

### Cited Findings
- Secretária IA appeared at Startup Summit 2026 (Jun 2026) — [Economia SC](https://economiasc.com/2026/06/30/healthtech-secretaria-ia-leva-ao-startup-summit-ia-que-aumenta-conversao-de-clinicas-medicas/)
- Healz Carol and FF.AI explicitly market compliance with the 2026 CFM AI resolution (2.454/2026) — [Healz](https://healz.com.br/produtos/secretaria-virtual-ia-para-medicos/); [FF.AI](https://ffai.ia.br/ia-para-whatsapp-de-clinica)
- Doutor-AI (founded around 2024–25; US$ 1M pre-seed) and Tolki (2026 press) are new entrants, mostly on the enterprise or clinic side — [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/17/04/2025/doutor-ai/); [Estado de Minas](https://www.em.com.br/mundo-corporativo/2026/10/7528143-tolki-capta-52-mil-pacientes-e-poupa-r-8-milhoes-a-clinicas.html)
- A Diário do Rio piece (31/08/2026) covers AI entering consultórios to give doctors back time lost to bureaucracy (trend coverage) — [Diário do Rio](https://www.diariodorio.com/saude/2026/08/31/ia-entra-nos-consultorios-e-promete-devolver-ao-medico-o-tempo-que-a-burocracia-tirou-do-atendimento.html)
- The big 2026 AI-for-doctors money went to scribes and copilots (Telepatia US$ 33M Series A), not to secretaries — [HiHub](https://hihub.news/2026/06/18/telepatia-ai-capta-us-33-milhoes-em-serie-a-liderada-pela-andreessen-horowitz-a16z/)

### Inferences
- Scribes are where the funding goes. Patient-facing AI secretaries remain bootstrapped or pre-seed, so no well-capitalised leader yet.
- A scribe player (Telepatia, Voa, Noa) or the DocPlanner group could move into patient-facing agents. That is the main consolidation risk.

### Gaps
- No M&A found. The search was not exhaustive (Crunchbase and LinkedIn were not accessible).

## Pricing mentioned (BRL)

### Takeaway
Dedicated AI secretaries for a solo doctor cluster around R$ 200–300/month plus setup. Generic chatbots run from R$ 50 to R$ 600/month, and incumbent AI add-ons cost about R$ 199–249.

### Cited Findings
- Syntia: R$ 247/month after a 7-day free trial (vendor page) — [Syntia](https://syntiamed.com/)
- Cloudia: base plan prices hidden. Setup "a partir de R$ 700". Add-ons are AI summary and reply suggestion at R$ 100/month, extra WhatsApp number at R$ 200/month, extra user at R$ 30/month. Annual discounts are 8–16% — [Cloudia planos](https://cloudia.com.br/planos/)
- Doctoralia Noa Notes add-on: R$ 199/month (pricing page; may be dated) — [Doctoralia preço](https://pro.doctoralia.com.br/preco)
- Feegow VIP plan with Noa Notes: R$ 249. iClinic includes WhatsApp from the Plus plan at R$ 129/month per professional (comparison blog by a competitor) — [doctte](https://www.doctte.com/blog/7-melhores-alternativas-ao-iclinic-e-feegow-em-2026-precos)
- Generic chatbots (zapfunil, Sep 2026):
  - ChatGuru Feegow automation: R$ 150/month.
  - BotConversa Pro: R$ 199/month.
  - Wati: R$ 299 / 599 / 1,449 per month.
  - SendPulse: about R$ 50/month.
  - ManyChat: about R$ 151/month.
  - Kommo: about R$ 468/month for 3 users.
  - Take Blip Feegow extension: R$ 299.90/month.
  - Source: [zapfunil](https://zapfunil.com/comparativos/melhores-chatbots-para-clinicas-no-whatsapp)

### Inferences
- A solo-doctor price anchor of about R$ 200–300/month is set by Syntia and the incumbent add-ons. Cloudia's R$ 700+ setup fee signals a clinic-oriented, implementation-heavy model.

### Gaps
- No public prices for Secretária IA, Clinia, Healz Carol, FF.AI or Axônio in the sources reviewed.
